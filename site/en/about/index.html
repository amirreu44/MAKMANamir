About

MAKMAN@ (Amir Reuven) is an emerging visual artist based in central Israel. working across painting, illustration, and music related projects. He has collaborated with artists in the underground rap scene, including Ohana and Bareket, Tochti, and Yoni Kli. Drawing on political undertones, Jewish heritage, personal experiences. His practice explores themes of identity, faith, and spirituality through expressive imagery, symbolism, and a distinctive visual language.
